Stop the trail at the source

Ants are one of the most common calls we get, especially once it warms up. Black house ants and coastal brown ants march through kitchens and pantries the moment they find food, and there are usually far more in the nest than the few you see on the bench.

Spraying the trail can actually make it worse, because some species split the colony and set up new nests when they are disturbed. We treat them the right way, targeting the nests and laying a barrier so they stop getting in.
