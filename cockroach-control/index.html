More than a nuisance

Cockroaches walk through drains and rubbish and then across your benches, spreading bacteria as they go. In a lot of homes they also make asthma and allergies worse, especially in kids. And they breed quickly, so a couple you spot at night can become an infestation before long.

The two we see most in Toowoomba are the small German cockroach, which loves warm kitchens and appliances, and the larger American cockroach that comes up through drains and subfloors. Each needs a slightly different approach, which is why a proper treatment beats a supermarket spray every time.

What we do

How we clear cockroaches

Signs you have a problem

  • Droppings that look like ground pepper or coffee grounds
  • A musty smell in cupboards or around appliances
  • Egg cases (small brown capsules) in corners and crevices
  • Seeing them scatter when you turn the light on at night

How we treat them

  • Gel baits placed where they harbour and feed
  • Residual treatment to the runs, cracks and entry points
  • Treating the source, not just the ones on show
  • Advice and follow-up so they stay gone, backed by a 12-month warranty
