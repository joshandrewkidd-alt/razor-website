Take your yard back

Toowoomba gets its share of spiders, and a treatment makes the difference between a home you can enjoy and one covered in webs. Webbing spiders like black house spiders set up around eaves, windows and fences. Ground-dwellers such as wolf spiders and the occasional funnel-web relative live in garden beds and around entries. And the ones that worry parents most, redbacks and white-tails, love sheds, outdoor furniture and dark corners.

We knock the webs down and lay a residual barrier so spiders that come back into contact with treated areas are controlled, not just scattered.

Spider numbers climb through spring and summer, so a treatment heading into the warmer months keeps them down when they are most active. Spider control is included in our general home treatment from $175.
