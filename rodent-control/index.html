Rats and mice are more than a nuisance

Rodents chew through wiring and pipes, which is a real fire and flood risk, they contaminate food and surfaces, and they can spread disease. Mice squeeze through tiny gaps and breed fast, while rats are stronger and often live in roofs, subfloors and around sheds. Both get worse as it cools down and they head indoors for winter.

We treat the problem safely and then help you seal the entry points, so you are not baiting the same rodents again next season.

What we do

How we deal with rodents

Signs of a rodent problem

  • Droppings in cupboards, drawers or the pantry
  • Gnaw marks on food packaging, cables or timber
  • Scratching or scurrying in the walls or roof at night
  • A musky smell, or nests made from shredded material

How we treat them

  • Tamper-resistant bait stations placed safely around pets and kids
  • Baiting targeted to where the rodents are actually active
  • Advice on sealing the entry points they use to get in
  • Follow-up so the problem is actually solved, not just quietened

Seal them out
