A general pest treatment for a standard home starts from $245 and covers ants, cockroaches, spiders and silverfish with a 12-month warranty. Units start from $155, termite inspections from $225. Final pricing depends on your property size and the pest, and your written quote is always free.

We use registered, low-toxicity products and follow the label safety directions, applied by a licensed technician. We'll let you know any simple steps for the day, like keeping pets and kids off a treated area until it's dry.

We usually get to you within the same week, and sooner for urgent jobs like wasp nests or rodents. Request a quote or give us a call and we'll book a time that suits.

General pest treatments come with a 12-month warranty: if pests return in that window, we come back and re-treat at no charge. Some pests like ants and rodents can have different terms, and we'll always explain the cover before we start.
