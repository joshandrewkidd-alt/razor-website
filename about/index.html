Your local pest control team

Razor Pest Control is a locally owned and operated business based in Toowoomba. We have been protecting homes and businesses across the city and the Darling Downs since 2012, and in that time we have completed more than 1,600 jobs, from one-off treatments to ongoing commercial programs.

We hold Queensland Pest Management Technician Licence 1003984313 and QBCC Licence 15343231, and we are fully insured with a certificate of currency available on request. Being local means we know the pests, the homes and the seasons around here, and we are only ever a short drive away when you need us.
